# Chessboard

A standalone chess practice site in `index.html`. Open the file in a modern
browser to play as White against the built-in computer as Black. No install,
account, server, or internet connection is required.

## Play

- Select a piece, then a highlighted square, or drag the piece to a legal square.
- Use **Computer strength** to choose Beginner, Casual, or Challenging.
- **Show a hint** highlights one move to consider.
- **Undo move** takes back your move and the computer's reply.
- **Flip board** changes the board orientation.
- Pawns that reach the last rank can promote to a queen, rook, bishop, or knight.

The game enforces legal moves, check, checkmate, stalemate, castling, en passant,
promotion, and the 50-move draw rule. The computer searches locally in the
browser; its strength and response time depend on the selected level and device.
