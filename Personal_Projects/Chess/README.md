# Chessboard

Open `index.html` in a modern browser to play chess against the built-in
computer. The app runs locally and does not require an account, server, or
internet connection.

## Features

- Play the computer at Easy, Medium, or Hard strength. The local
  engine uses iterative deepening, alpha-beta search, a transposition table,
  tactical capture search, and positional evaluation.
- Set an untimed game or use 1-minute, 3-minute with 2-second increment,
  5-minute, or 10-minute clocks.
- Create local player profiles. Ratings, game results, lesson progress, and
  puzzle progress are saved in this browser on this device only. These are
  local profiles, not secure or online accounts; do not store private
  information in them. Ratings are local Elo-style estimates against a
  computer rated at 1000, not official or online ratings.
- Study five short lessons and answer their review questions.
- Solve three checkmate-in-one puzzles on the board.
- Claim a draw after threefold repetition or the 50-move rule. Fivefold
  repetition, the 75-move rule, stalemate, and insufficient material are also
  detected.
- Make legal moves by selecting or dragging pieces, promote pawns, undo a
  move and the CPU reply, flip the board, and request a move hint.
- After a CPU game ends, choose **Review game** to compare your moves with
  local engine suggestions. The review labels move quality and highlights
  useful alternatives and tactical mistakes. Analysis stays in your browser.

All game play, profiles, lessons, puzzles, and CPU calculations are local to
the browser. There is no online multiplayer, cloud account, or server-based
rating. Browser storage may be cleared by the browser or lost when using
private browsing.
